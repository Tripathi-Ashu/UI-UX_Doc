## About My Trainer

**Deepika Sharma** is a Senior UI/UX Designer in the Technology department at Nirvana Lab, and she was my trainer for this phase.

Under her guidance, I learned:

- UI and UX principles, from user research and user flows to visual hierarchy and layout.
- Figma in practice: frames, auto layout, components, variants and prototyping.
- How to think about the user journey before designing the final screen.
- How to build a consistent design system and apply it across screens.

Her feedback on my wireframes, components and prototype helped me improve the quality of the final project.

:::info
I am grateful to my trainer for the guidance on UI/UX principles and Figma, and to my project manager for the direction and feedback that shaped the final project.
:::

## Objectives

- Build a strong foundation in UI and UX principles.
- Gain hands-on proficiency in Figma, including auto layout, components, variants and styles.
- Follow the complete design process, from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity UI.
- Create a reusable design system covering color, typography and components.
- Design and prototype a real-world mobile application end to end.
