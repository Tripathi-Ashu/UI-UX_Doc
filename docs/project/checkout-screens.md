# Checkout Screen (11)

## 11 · Checkout

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/11-checkout.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/11-checkout.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Order complete karwana, kam se kam steps mein.

## Sections

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| **Delivery address** | Naam, address type (Home), poora address, phone number, Change option |
| **Order items (3)** | Product image, size, quantity, price, Edit bag |
| **Payment method** | UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, UPI ID verify), Credit/Debit card, Net banking, Cash on delivery |
| **Price details** | Product price, Discount, Coupon discount, Delivery charges, **Total amount** |
| **Savings message** | "You are saving $1,800 on this order" |
| **Safe and secure checkout** | Payment encrypted, purchase protection, 7-day returns |
| **Sticky footer** | Total payable aur **Place order** button |

## Why this design?

- **UPI default selected** hai kyunki Indian users mein sabse popular hai.
- Saare payment options ek screen par hain, extra page nahi kholna padta.
- **Savings message** user ko khushi deta hai aur order cancel hone ke chance kam karta hai.
- **Secure checkout note** trust banata hai.
- Cash on delivery ka extra fee pehle se dikhaya gaya hai, taaki koi surprise na ho.

## Learnings

- Checkout screen par distractions kam hone chahiye: isliye bottom navigation hata diya gaya.
- Primary button hamesha sticky rakhne se conversion behtar hota hai.

# Checkout Screen (11)

## 11 · Checkout

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/11-checkout.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/11-checkout.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Complete the purchase in as few steps as possible.

## Sections

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| **Delivery address** | Name, address type, full address, phone number and a Change option |
| **Order items** | Product thumbnail, size, quantity, price and an Edit bag option |
| **Payment method** | UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and UPI ID verification), Credit or Debit card, Net banking and Cash on delivery |
| **Price details** | Product price, discount, coupon discount, delivery charges and total amount |
| **Savings message** | A green confirmation of the amount saved on the order |
| **Secure checkout note** | Assurance of encrypted payments, purchase protection and returns |
| **Sticky footer** | Total payable and the **Place order** button |

## Design Rationale

- **UPI is preselected** because it is the most widely used payment method among the target users.
- All payment options appear on one screen, so users do not navigate to extra pages.
- The **savings message** reinforces the value of the purchase and reduces last-minute hesitation.
- The **secure checkout note** builds trust at the moment of payment.
- Extra Cash on Delivery charges are shown up front to avoid surprises.
- The selected payment option uses a soft saffron tint, making the current choice unmistakable.

## Key Takeaways

- Distractions are minimized at checkout, so the bottom navigation is removed.
- A sticky primary button keeps the final action always within reach.
