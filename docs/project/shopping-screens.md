# Shopping Screens (06–10)

Ye app ka core hai: product dhoondhna, save karna aur bag mein daalna.

## 06 · Home

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/06-home.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/06-home.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Products discover karwana.
**Key elements:**
- Location selector aur notification bell
- Search bar (voice aur camera search ke saath)
- Tabs: All, Men, Women, Kids
- Category chips: Top Wear, Bottom Wear, Footwear, Accessories, Bags, Sports
- Hero banner (sale / festival offer)
- Sections: Deals of the Day (countdown timer), Trending Now, Top Brands, Recommended for you
- Bottom navigation

**Why this design?** Content importance ke order mein hai: pehle search, phir categories, phir offers. Countdown timer urgency banata hai, aur "See all" har section ko expandable rakhta hai.

## 07 · Profile

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/07-profile.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/07-profile.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Account aur settings ek jagah.
**Key elements:** Name, number, Edit button; Coupons, Help center, Orders ke quick tiles; Wallet and credits (₹250); Account list (Saved addresses, Payment methods, Returns and refunds); Support list; Log out.

**Why this design?** Sabse zyada use hone wale options (Orders, Coupons) upar bade tiles mein hain. Baaki cheezein Account aur Support groups mein baanti gayi hain taaki list saaf rahe.

## 08 · Wishlist

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/08-wishlist.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/08-wishlist.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Pasandida products save karna.
**Key elements:** Item count (6 items), Sort aur Filter, 2-column grid, rating badge, heart icon, price aur discount, **Move to Bag** button.

**Why this design?** 2-column grid mein ek saath zyada products dikhte hain. Move to Bag button se user bina product page khole kharidari ki taraf badh sakta hai.

## 09 · Bag

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/09-bag.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/09-bag.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Chune hue products review karna.
**Key elements:** Delivery address, item list (size, price, discount, delivery date), quantity stepper, delete icon, Apply coupon, **Price details** (Bag total, Discount, Coupon, Delivery, Total), sticky total aur **Place order** button.

**Why this design?** Price breakup transparent hai, isse user ka bharosa badhta hai. Total aur Place order neeche sticky hai, isliye scroll karne par bhi dikhte hain.

## 10 · Product Detail

| Wireframe | Final UI |
|---|---|
| <img src="/img/wireframes/10-product-detail.png" width="250" /> | <img src="/img/final-ui/10-product-detail.png" width="250" /> |

**Purpose:** Product ki poori jaankari dena aur kharidne ka decision aasan banana.
**Key elements:** Image carousel, share aur wishlist icons, rating, brand aur name, price (MRP, discount, "inclusive of all taxes"), color selector, size selector (S–XXL) aur Size chart, delivery check, Cash on delivery aur 7-day returns info, Similar products, **Add to bag** aur **Buy Now**.

**Why this design?** Decision lene ke liye zaroori info (size, delivery, returns) ek hi screen par hai. Do CTAs: Add to bag (secondary) aur Buy Now (primary black) alag-alag user intent ko serve karte hain.
