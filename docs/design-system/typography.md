# Typography

The interface uses a clean sans-serif typeface (Inter) chosen for its legibility on mobile screens and its modern, neutral character.




## Type Scale

| Style | Size | Weight | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heading 1** | 24 px | Semi Bold | Screen titles such as "Login or sign up" and "Checkout" |
| **Heading 2** | 18 px | Semi Bold | Section titles such as "Deals of the Day" and "Trending Now" |
| **Body** | 14 px | Regular | Descriptions, addresses and list items |
| **Body Strong** | 14 px | Medium | Product names, labels and button text |
| **Price** | 16–18 px | Bold | Selling price |
| **Caption** | 12 px | Regular | Helper text, delivery information, MRP and ratings |

## Guidelines

- Limit each screen to a **maximum of three text sizes**.
- Display the selling price in **bold** and the original price as **grey strikethrough**.
- Use medium-weight white text on filled buttons.
- Keep line height at roughly **1.4× the font size** for readability.
